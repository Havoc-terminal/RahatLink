Connecting Help to Those Who Need It

RahatLink bridges the gap between donors and people in need during disasters. Fast, efficient, and life-saving.

About RahatLink

RahatLink is a smart disaster relief platform that connects NGOs, donors, and affected people. It ensures resources reach the right place at the right time, reducing waste and saving lives.

How It Works

1. Post Resources

Donors and NGOs add available supplies.

2. Request Needs

People can request help based on urgency.

3. Smart Matching

System connects needs with best resources.
